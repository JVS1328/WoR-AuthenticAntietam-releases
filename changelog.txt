Authentic Antietam 1.2.1 - officers' rank insignia and gunners' forearms

- Fixed officers being offered another rank's insignia. A 2nd lieutenant could wear a 1st lieutenant's two collar bars, a captain a lieutenant's, and so on. About 650 wardrobe items were affected. Every officer's choices now carry only their own rank's insignia, including a few the base game itself had wrong (1st Virginia Cavalry lieutenants, a Balthis lieutenant colonel).
- Fixed shirt-sleeve gunners in 32 battery crews having no forearms, their hands floating at the rolled-up sleeve. They now have bare forearms like the game's own shirt-sleeve men.
- Still client-side only: you can join normal servers, and other players see the vanilla game.

Built for the War of Rights patch of November 4, 2026 (Steam build 26426403).
